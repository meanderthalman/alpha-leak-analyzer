The Time-Delay Tax

Calculates exactly how many years you must "work extra" or stay invested in a benchmark to recover the capital lost to underperformance.

The Inflation Paradox

When "Inflation Adjusted" is ON, the numerical leak shrinks because future money is discounted. However, the time delay increases because your "Real" recovery rate is slower.

Stochastic Modeling

This tool uses Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) to simulate market volatility, ensuring performance paths mimic real-world market movements.
